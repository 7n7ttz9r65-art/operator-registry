Glossary — what the words mean

The verdicts

REJECTED — a fixed rejection rule fired: this construction is a proven no-go.
CONDITIONAL_SURVIVOR — the construction works if its named (still unproved) assumptions turn out to be true.
UNCERTIFIED_INDETERMINATE — not enough certified evidence to say either way yet. Often this means negative results from ordinary floating-point computation, which can never be trusted as proof.
UNCONDITIONAL_SURVIVOR — every required property proved with valid certification. None exist yet.
SCHEMA_VIOLATION — the record itself is malformed, so it cannot be judged at all.

Certification levels — how much to trust a status

none — no evidence yet.
floating_point — ordinary computer arithmetic. Suggestive, but rounding error means it can never count as proof.
interval_arithmetic — rigorous computation: every step carries certified error bounds.
analytic_proof — established by mathematical derivation, no computation required.

The 7 properties, in plain English

trace — does the operator's diagonal sum reproduce the prime arithmetic exactly?
positivity — are all its energies non-negative, as a valid quantum Hamiltonian must be?
covariance — is its transformation behavior consistent with the arithmetic it has to reproduce?
self_adjoint — is it equal to its own adjoint, the requirement for real measurement outcomes?
compact_resolvent — does it have the right kind of discrete spectrum?
weyl — do its eigenvalues follow the right growth law?
determinant — does its determinant encode the target zeros?

Why certification matters: a "proved" claim backed only by floating-point numbers is exactly the error this registry exists to catch. The engine will refuse it.
